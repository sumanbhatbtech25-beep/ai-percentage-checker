    Research Papers

    Find the 5 papers closest to your work

    • Upload your report or type a topic
    • Searches OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar, which cover 250M+ works
    • Results ranked on this computer by how close their meaning is to your report
    • Shows authors, year, citations, the abstract and free PDFs when available

    3. ResearchFind related papers to read and cite.
